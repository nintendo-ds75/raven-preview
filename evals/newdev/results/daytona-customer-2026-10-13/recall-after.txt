Mira Security decided to use 'None-falls-through' semantics: when a caller passes max_age=None explicitly (or omits max_age entirely) to loads() or loads_unsafe(), the serializer's configured default_max_age is used instead. Only a numeric per-call max_age overrides the default. No sentinel value is introduced, and TimestampSigner is left unchanged. The rationale was: 'Callers that opt into a serializer age policy should not disable it accidentally by forwarding None.'

This is an evidence reading. It creates no decision, signature or authorization.
