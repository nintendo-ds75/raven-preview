# Contact learning contract

This follow-up changes contact observations and rankings only. It does not change authorization, required signers, owner eligibility, authority-map acceptance, or approval lifetimes. Tests use unique synthetic contacts and no providers.

- A committed referral retains an ordered observation with stable decision/person IDs, from/to people, an explicit outcome, the supplied reason, and a snapshot of repository, question, category, path, and facts. A committed answer retains its responding contact. An ordinary owner confirmation retains the distinct `owner_confirmed` outcome: the owner confirmed an existing answer, without claiming to have authored it. Original observations survive later handoffs and responses.
- Referral alone is not rejection. After a scoped chain actually reaches an answering or confirming contact, that terminal contact ranks first among learned contacts; intermediaries that connect to the response are labeled helpful connectors and rank afterward. Explicitly recorded unsuitability is immediately usable contrary evidence, even if its new referral is pending, disconnected, or looping; it does not earn connector credit. Silence, reminders, and escalations supply neither refusal nor expertise.
- Contacts are reusable only within the existing repository, question/topic, path, and fact matching rules. Department, organization, and domain apply only when actually supplied as facts. One answer creates no broader contact expertise.
- A declared `work_item` can differ for contact reuse only when both declarations unambiguously match their own persisted canonical task work-item anchors. Same-item links strengthen relevance; all material and other scope facts still constrain the match. The [typed-context contract](contact-context-contract.md) specifies the exact pin, declaration and legacy rules. Answer applicability is unchanged.
- Any explicit temporary/one-question learning opt-out excludes all observations of that decision at read time, even observations made before the opt-out. No positive connector credit is derived from incomplete, disconnected, self-referring, or looping referral paths. Explicit declines keep their independent scope, freshness, review-state, and opt-out filters.
- Contact evidence has a bounded 180-day freshness window measured from the source occurrence; future-dated or malformed timestamps are ineligible. Within the window, similarity and recency order contacts; newer matching contrary evidence overrides an earlier success. This ages contact suggestions only, never approvals or authority.
- The append-only contact trail is idempotent per committed source revision. Duplicate boundary callbacks do not create additional evidence, refresh evidence age, or multiply ranking weight.
- Legacy routing feedback remains explicitly legacy. Existing answered/declined records can contribute only with scope, freshness, current review state, and opt-out checks; no structured historical edge is reconstructed from names or prose.

Observable outputs: contact candidates and ranked contact evidence distinguish authored answers, owner confirmations, connectors, and explicit declines; contact_observed events expose ordered stable identities and scope snapshots. Existing authority precedence remains unchanged. Historical `owner_confirmed` evidence grants no current ownership, signing eligibility, or authority.

## Freshness and ordering choices

`CONTACT_FRESH_DAYS = 180` is a local product policy, not an external standard. Age is measured from the source occurrence timestamp, in UTC. Age zero and exactly 180 days are eligible; any future timestamp, unknown timestamp, or age greater than 180 days is ineligible. Original timestamps are immutable on duplicate processing. Source revision, occurrence time, local observation time, and chain sequence are stored separately. Connectors use their own referral occurrence time; a later answer or correction never rejuvenates an old connector.

Answering and confirming contacts sort before connectors. Within each group, the existing question-term similarity and any bounded typed-relationship bonus are multiplied by `1 - 0.25 * age_days / CONTACT_FRESH_DAYS` and the typed-context confidence factor; equally similar fresher contacts therefore sort first. The [typed-context contract](contact-context-contract.md) specifies bonuses and confidence. Person name and stable ID break ties. The newest matching outcome for a person wins before scoring, so a later explicit decline supersedes an older success. Repetition does not accumulate weight.

The initial red test expected an alphabetical connector order (Alpha before Beta). That original assertion and output are preserved separately; the [public verification report](../evals/results/contact-learning-2026-10-07/README.md) summarizes the initial failures. The completed-chain test now asserts the independent contract: Gamma responds first, and both Alpha and Beta remain available as connectors. A separate controlled-time test checks the documented freshness rule, with a 10-day connector ahead of an equally similar 30-day connector.

## Input and compatibility details

A normal committed referral has outcome `referred`. An explicit caller-supplied `contact_outcome: "declined"` records that the outgoing contact is unsuitable for this scope; the storage boundary never derives refusal from its note. Conversation proposals require the visible confirmation described below. The note is retained as the observation reason. Existing `scope_kind: "none"`/`"this"` events opt out the entire decision. This metadata grants no authority and changes no authorization check.

The legacy `routing_feedback` table stays for compatibility, including its old forwarded-away `declined` value. Where structured observations exist, contact ranking ignores compatibility declines, which only meant forwarded-away. If the decision has no structured answer or owner-confirmation observation, a legacy answered row can still supply a labeled legacy respondent; it does not fabricate a terminal edge or credit the structured connectors. If either structured terminal response exists, its chain supplies positive evidence and compatibility answered rows are ignored. A legacy row alone remains explicitly labeled legacy; its historical referral sequence is unknown. Repeating an identical legacy record does not refresh its timestamp. A genuinely new answer through a structured boundary creates a new observation.

Existing typed task anchors and active decision `work_item`/`context` sources are retained with task/client reference, record/version IDs, provider, source namespace, object kind, external ID, source update/observation timestamps, and source sequence when available. The [typed-context contact contract](contact-context-contract.md) now compares canonical source namespaces and material facts, and uses shared work-item/source identities for bounded relevance bonuses. Different work items can generalize in compatible scope. A host need not provide opaque IDs. Organization/domain separation requires explicit facts; unknown historical scope is labeled and downweighted, and unstructured prose does not establish it. These remain contact suggestions, not organization-wide discovery or authority.

### Owner confirmations

Future unchanged-answer UI sign-offs and ordinary Slack confirmations share the successful `canvas.sign_off` writer. Only its existing `basis == 'owner'` path with no required-signers list and no outstanding signers records `owner_confirmed`. The established authorization basis is consumed as-is. Partial or multi-approver completion remains outside this structured hook. A learning opt-out prevents a new confirmation observation, and a later opt-out excludes the whole prior chain at read time.

The observation and `contact_observed` event commit in the same transaction as the successful sign-off, after source and revision checks. Its `source_revision` is the immutable committed decision-version ID, also retained under `source_context.confirmation.decision_version_id`. That version contains the exact answer, signatures, scope and all source/derivation pins. The observation separately captures current task facts and typed task/decision context. Its occurrence time is the sign-off timestamp, independent of both the immutable revision ID and local observation time. No answer author, source, signature decision, or authority is changed by this hook.

Deduplication is deliberately conservative. A fingerprint of the answer, rationale, authorship/source fields, explicit scope, immutable source/derivation pins, and captured task context is compared with the latest confirmation for that decision and contact. An identical callback or repeated unchanged confirmation keeps the original observation and contact age even if sign-off rewrites `updated_at`, signature bookkeeping, or decision-version IDs. The same unchanged review after expiry does not renew contact evidence. A changed effective response or material context can create a new observation; distinct immutable source revisions remain distinct even when occurrence timestamps are equal. Chain sequence resolves those tied times. Earlier observations and connector ages remain intact. This applies only to newly committed confirmations. Old legacy sign-off feedback stays legacy; existing structured answers and referrals remain structured and usable under their existing contract. No old rows are rewritten or backfilled.

Task facts and task anchors can change while both the decision version and occurrence time stay equal. Only `owner_confirmed` observation identity therefore also includes the material fingerprint and previous confirmation ID, retained as `source_context.confirmation.previous_observation_id`. A context transition A → B → A records all three responses in order; repeating the current A still returns without a new observation or age refresh. `source_revision` and `confirmation.decision_version_id` remain genuine immutable decision-version IDs, and occurrence time stays separate. Existing authored-answer and referral observation ID recipes are unchanged.

The internal confirmation callback also requires its immutable version to match the existing current decision binding. Replaying an obsolete version and its original occurrence time after a newer response writes nothing; it cannot append old material at the end of the contact trail. This is a source-version freshness check only and does not recompute signature or owner eligibility.

## Limits

The structured hooks cover committed `Store.refer`, `Store.answer`, and the ordinary owner-confirmation boundary described above. Other existing answering flows can contribute labeled legacy feedback when there is no structured terminal response for that decision. A later legacy-only answer after a structured response is conservatively omitted because the mutable legacy row cannot reliably distinguish a new source occurrence from a callback retry. Existing independent authority/inference signals keep their separate semantics. Contact freshness and contact opt-out do not expire or revoke those independent signals. No historical structured chain is backfilled, no external data is fetched, and no contact is messaged by learning. Observations are atomic with their source transaction; a storage failure rolls back that transaction rather than committing an unaudited observation.

Weak `source=answer`, `role=knows` rows are a second representation of answer-derived learning, not independent routing evidence. The legacy signal scorer excludes these rows; the existing structured or explicitly labeled legacy contact history determines whether an answer supplies a contact suggestion. The stored rows remain unchanged. A weak row with no usable contact history supplies no recipient, and its free-text note is never parsed to invent a historical decision link. Explicitly configured routing maps and declared learned referrals retain their separate scope and behavior. This boundary changes only routing consumption, not authority-map storage, signature eligibility, required signers, or approval state.

When the canvas reuses an answer, its historical signer can be prioritized as a recipient only if the current combined routing result already includes that person. This is a recipient-nomination condition in `canvas._route_signer`, after current contact applicability, contrary outcomes, opt-out and freshness have been evaluated. An empty ranking cannot revive a declined, expired, opted-out or foreign-scope answer-derived contact. Independent applicable routing evidence can still nominate the person. This clarifies contact freshness for nomination; it does not expire the historical signature or change signature lookup, validation, eligibility, authority, required approvers, or approval lifetime. Answer-cache applicability and displayed answer status remain separate from recipient selection.

An applicable explicit contact decline also excludes that person from named-record and topical-record author fallbacks after Slack reachability filtering. A decline from an incompatible scope does not exclude the person. Explicit independent decider-map precedence stays unchanged. These are recipient checks with synthetic stored directory reachability; they do not change messaging, identity resolution, or authority.


## Explicit unsuitability in conversation

The conversation action schema accepts `contact_outcome` as blank, `referred`, or `declined`; `declined` is valid only for handoffs. The prompt restricts it to the speaker explicitly saying they are unsuitable as a contact for similar questions in this scope, not ordinary forwarding, “not me,” another person's wording, silence, or absence. The contact field applies only when the speaker is the outgoing contact; an allowed handoff by someone else remains an ordinary referral. The proposed action visibly reads back: “Remember that you are not a suitable first contact for similar questions in this scope. This changes contact suggestions only.” The existing confirmation flow carries that exact metadata to the referral boundary. No contact observation is written merely by offering the read-back. Temporary-cover interpretation resets the outcome to an ordinary referral and keeps the existing whole-question learning opt-out.

Synthetic schema/read-back/apply checks validate representation and plumbing. No live model classification or alternative answer/signature authorization path was tested. Within one structured chain, source sequence resolves tied occurrence timestamps; this prevents an earlier decline from defeating a later same-time respondent observation.
